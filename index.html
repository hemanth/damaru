Damaru डमरु · 3D Acoustic Physics

The damaru is an ancient two-headed hourglass pressure drum from India and the Himalayas. Rotating the narrow waist whips two knotted cords so their hard lac beads (goli) strike opposing goatskin membranes in rapid alternation, while squeezing the V-laced waist cords raises membrane tension and pitch.

  • Drag / Shake Drag or wiggle horizontally or vertically — faster wrist velocity whips both beaded cords harder and faster across the heads.
  • Tap / Click Strike either goatskin head directly (center for warm resonant Dam, rim for crisp Ta).
  • Hold Space Or pinch with two fingers to cinch the V-laced waist cords and bend pitch upward in real time.
  • Phone / Keys Shake your phone physically, or press F / J, ← / →, R (roll), and A (auto play).
